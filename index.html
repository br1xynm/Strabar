The concept

Not a map of bars. Your night, traced.

Strabar records the real route of your night out.

Track your route

Turn on GPS when you head out and let Strabar draw your route in real time.

Log every stop

Mark the places you visit and keep count of your night, your way.

Discover other routes

Browse the routes the community has taken around your city.

Share the experience

Turn your Strabar into a card that's ready to share with your friends.

How it works

From your front door to the stories you'll tell

Four steps, designed to never get in the way of your night.

1

Go out

Open Strabar the moment the plan starts. No friction, no forms.

2

Track your route

GPS traces your path while you focus on the night itself.

3

Discover

Browse other users' routes and get ideas for your next night out.

4

Explore more city

Every finished Strabar unlocks new neighbourhoods, spots and recommendations.
